How it works

  • Linear Regression — 5 patient features with literature-based weights → continuous risk score (0–100).
  • Logistic Regression — sigmoid function on score → binary High/Low classification with probability.
  • Cosine Similarity — matches diagnosis text against 10 care pathway profiles.
